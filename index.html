Singing Bowl

Documentation

Singing Bowl is a small generative instrument that synthesises the sound of a bronze bowl from slightly detuned sine partials. Strike it, listen to the overtones bloom and shape the tone until it resonates with you.

Quick start

  1. Press the round Strike button. The first strike also unlocks audio playback in the browser.
  2. Set a comfortable level with Master volume.
  3. Open Fine tuning to adjust partials, gains and decay times.

Controls

Strike
Plays one strike. Striking again retriggers the bowl and softly damps the previous sound.
Master volume
Output level from −40 to 0 dBFS. The slider and the number field stay in sync; edits take effect immediately.
Fine tuning
Opens the four partial groups. Every change inside is applied live and saved automatically.
Partial switch
Turns a single sine partial on or off. Disabled partials are dimmed and are skipped by the audio engine.
Frequency
Pitch of a partial in hertz. Values are applied to a running tone live. Slightly detuned neighbours create the characteristic slow beating.
Partial gain
Level of one partial from −60 to 0 dBFS.
Group gain
Level of all partials in a group from −60 to 0 dBFS.
Decay time
How long a group rings after a strike, from 0.5 to 12 seconds. Longer times suit the low fundamental groups; shorter times keep the upper partials clean.

How the sound is made

The instrument uses additive synthesis with the Web Audio API. Every partial is an oscillator with its own frequency, level and envelope. Small frequency offsets between partials produce the slow amplitude beating typical of a singing bowl.

  • A short filtered noise burst models the mallet contact.
  • An attack and decay envelope shapes every strike.
  • A limiter at the output prevents clipping when many partials add up.
  • All parameters are applied live to the running sound.

Saved settings

Master volume, partial frequencies, gains, switch states, group gains and decay times are stored in your browser's local storage. They are restored automatically the next time the app starts.

Settings never leave your device. Clearing this site's data in the browser resets everything to the defaults.

Install and offline use

Singing Bowl is a Progressive Web App. When served from http://localhost or over HTTPS, Chrome and Edge offer an Install button in the header. On iOS Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen.

A service worker caches the app shell after the first visit, so the instrument keeps working offline. New versions are picked up automatically when you are online again.

Tips

  • Start with the default tuning and lower the upper partial gains for a softer, more meditative tone.
  • Long decay times on the low groups and short times on the high groups keep the sound open and clear.
  • Very small frequency differences (a fraction of a hertz) create the strongest beating.
  • Retrigger the bowl while it is still ringing to hear the damping transition.

Troubleshooting

No sound
Strike once to unlock audio, check the master volume and make sure the system output is not muted.
Install button is missing
The app must run on localhost or HTTPS. Some browsers do not offer installation on desktop; use the browser menu instead.
Settings are not restored
Private windows and blocked site storage can prevent saving. Allow local storage for this site or leave private browsing.
The sound is distorted
Lower the master volume or reduce the gains of the loudest partials. The built-in limiter protects the output, but very high levels still sound compressed.
